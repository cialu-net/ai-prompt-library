# Research

## Investigate a question

```text
Investigate [QUESTION] for [AUDIENCE]. Define the scope and key terms, summarize the strongest evidence on each relevant side, and distinguish established findings from uncertainty. Cite accessible primary sources with dates and links where available. Do not invent citations; say when you cannot verify a claim.
```

## Evaluate a claim

```text
Evaluate this claim: [CLAIM]. Break it into testable assertions, identify evidence needed, consider alternative explanations, and explain what would change your conclusion. Clearly label assumptions and cite sources if available.
```
