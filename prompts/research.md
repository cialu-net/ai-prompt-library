# Research

## Investigate a question

```text
Investigate [QUESTION] for [AUDIENCE]. Define the scope and key terms, summarize the strongest evidence on each relevant side, and distinguish established findings from uncertainty. Cite accessible primary sources with dates and links where available. Do not invent citations; say when you cannot verify a claim.
```

## Evaluate a claim

```text
Evaluate this claim: [CLAIM]. Break it into testable assertions, identify evidence needed, consider alternative explanations, and explain what would change your conclusion. Clearly label assumptions and cite sources if available.
```

## Compare sources

```text
Compare these sources on [QUESTION]: [SOURCES OR EXCERPTS]. For each, identify its author, date, evidence, methods, limitations, and potential conflicts of interest when available. Summarize agreements and disagreements in a table, distinguishing what each source says from your assessment. Flag missing information and do not invent citations.
```

## Create a research plan

```text
Create a research plan for [QUESTION] for [AUDIENCE] within [TIME OR RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS]. Break the question into answerable subquestions, suggest suitable primary sources and search terms, and define how to assess evidence quality. List likely gaps and a stopping criterion. Do not claim the research has already been completed.
```
