# Productivity

## Turn a goal into a plan

```text
Help me plan [GOAL] by [DEADLINE]. Available time and resources: [RESOURCES]. Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]. Break the work into prioritized steps with realistic milestones, dependencies, and the next action. Highlight assumptions and potential obstacles.
```

## Decision framework

```text
Help me choose between [OPTIONS] for [DECISION]. My criteria are [CRITERIA] and constraints are [CONSTRAINTS]. Compare the options in a table, identify trade-offs and uncertainties, and suggest a reversible next step to gather information. Explain how the recommendation changes if the assumptions change.
```

## Prioritize a task list

```text
Help me prioritize these tasks: [TASKS]. My goals are [GOALS], available time is [TIME], and deadlines or dependencies are [CONSTRAINTS]. Suggest an order with a brief reason for each item, identify what can be deferred, and propose a realistic first work session. Ask about any missing information that would materially change the order.
```

## Prepare for a meeting

```text
Help me prepare for a meeting about [TOPIC] with [ATTENDEES]. The desired outcome is [OUTCOME] and the time available is [DURATION]. Draft a timed agenda, the most important questions, and any preparation needed. Separate known facts from assumptions and identify decisions that need an owner.
```
