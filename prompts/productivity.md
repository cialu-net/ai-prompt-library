# Productivity

## Turn a goal into a plan

```text
Help me plan [GOAL] by [DEADLINE]. Available time and resources: [RESOURCES]. Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]. Break the work into prioritized steps with realistic milestones, dependencies, and the next action. Highlight assumptions and potential obstacles.
```

## Decision framework

```text
Help me choose between [OPTIONS] for [DECISION]. My criteria are [CRITERIA] and constraints are [CONSTRAINTS]. Compare the options in a table, identify trade-offs and uncertainties, and suggest a reversible next step to gather information. Explain how the recommendation changes if the assumptions change.
```
