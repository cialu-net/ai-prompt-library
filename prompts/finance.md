# Finance

## Compare financial scenarios

Use this to understand the assumptions behind a financial decision.

```text
Compare these options: [OPTIONS]. My goal is [GOAL], time horizon is [HORIZON], and constraints are [CONSTRAINTS]. Create a table of assumptions, potential benefits, costs, risks, and situations where each option may be unsuitable. Show illustrative calculations and explain their limitations. Identify missing information and questions to ask a qualified adviser. Do not invent current rates or tax rules; cite reliable sources if available.
```

## Explain a financial statement

```text
Explain the following financial statement for a non-expert: [PASTE REDACTED STATEMENT]. Identify key figures, trends, and questions to investigate. Separate facts from interpretation, show calculations, and flag missing context. Do not infer figures that are not provided.
```

## Build a monthly budget

Use this to organize income and expenses into a workable plan.

```text
Help me build a monthly budget from [INCOME], [FIXED EXPENSES], [VARIABLE EXPENSES], [DEBTS], and [GOALS]. Use [CURRENCY] and the periods supplied. Calculate totals and the remaining balance, then suggest realistic adjustments in priority order. Flag missing or inconsistent figures and show how irregular expenses could be accounted for. Do not assume rates or taxes.
```

## Stress-test a budget

```text
Stress-test this budget: [BUDGET]. Model [SCENARIOS, SUCH AS LOWER INCOME OR HIGHER EXPENSES] over [TIME PERIOD]. Show the assumptions, monthly impact and cumulative shortfall or surplus for each scenario. Suggest possible responses and questions to investigate; do not present uncertain projections as guarantees.
```
